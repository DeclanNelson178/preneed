    What this does not do

    Every item below is a decision, not a defect. Read them before you act on a number.

    • MortalityEvery year of death is shown as a curve. The years are not weighted by how likely each one is, and there is no single expected value.
    • Non-cost-protected contractsThe family pays the difference, so there is no funeral-home economics to model.
    • Instalment trustsA part-funded trust is not cost-protected under 239 CMR 4.01.
    • Cash advance itemsBilled to the family at the time of service. 239 CMR 4.08(6)(c) and (d) are out of scope.
    • The tax engineOne measured effective rate replaces it. See docs/LAW.md section 7.
    • Lapse and commission chargebackA real risk on a multi-pay plan. It stays with you.
    • Accidental death benefitMost policies pay full face for accidental death inside the waiting period. Years 1 and 2 are therefore slightly pessimistic on a graded product.
    • Sequence of returnsThe return is a fixed rate, not a market path. A fall that lands in a claim year is not modelled.
    • Working capital239 CMR 4.08(5) requires a death certificate and a certification of performance before payment. You carry the case for some weeks.
    • Contract cancellation239 CMR 4.07 governs cancellation and refunds. Not modelled.

    Read this before you use it

    This is a calculator, not advice. Every starting value is a placeholder. Replace each one with your trustee statement, your general price list, your carrier illustration and your accountant's rate. The status mark beside each field says how far the starting value can be trusted.
